Finding your calendar's iCal address

  1. Open Google Calendar on the web.
  2. In the left sidebar, hover the calendar you want to import, click the three-dot menu, then choose Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to Integrate calendar.
  4. Copy the Secret address in iCal format — not the public address, so private event details still come through.
  5. Paste it into the field here.

Treat this address like a password — anyone who has it can see this calendar's events.
